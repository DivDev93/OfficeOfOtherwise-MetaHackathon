Keep separate rounds separate. The planned pilot stays at 3–5 observed creators, at least 80% scoring 4/5 or higher, and zero mistaken source-verification beliefs. A small pilot does not establish broad demand or learning impact.

YOUR SESSION ·

Try a world. Tell us what holds up.

We are trying an early creative tool, not testing you. This session is for adults who write, build worlds or make concept art. You can skip a question or stop at any time. No audio or screen recording is needed.

The saved stories are what-ifs. Linked sources support starting-world facts; changed paths are imagined. The 3D object is an illustration.

Your choices

Quotation permission is optional. If you decline notes, you can still explore the replay without returning a result.

READ THIS BEFORE STARTING

Your six-minute task

  1. Open a saved what-if that interests you. Choose one path and compare another.
  2. Use one linked source to name a starting-world fact and its source. Identify the changed assumption, two imagined steps and one uncertainty.
  3. Inspect the map, scene or 3D object. Name one concrete visual detail that helps or distracts from your concept.
  4. Save a scenario brief, open it and check that it preserves the source, assumption and uncertainty.

Please say what you are thinking if someone is observing. Ask for help whenever you need it. Six minutes is a target; you may continue and report the extra time.
