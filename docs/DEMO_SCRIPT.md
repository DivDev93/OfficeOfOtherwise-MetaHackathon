## Narration and production disclosure

Narration uses local CPU inference with **Kokoro-82M** (`onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX`, voice `af_heart`, fp32, speed 1.0) solely for offline video production outside the Meta-powered application. It is synthetic narration, not Divakar Dev’s voice. Sixteen sentences (`319` words) were synthesized separately at `24 kHz` with explicit `120 ms` inter-sentence gaps; SRT and VTT captions derive from measured PCM sample counts and align to the edited chapter timeline. The six UI chapters are edited saved-result replays, not a continuous live-generation session. The source was an isolated local Chrome native recording at 25 fps; the final MP4 is H.264 at 25 fps (`yuv420p`) with AAC audio at 48 kHz. Chapters 07 and 08 are authored 1920×1080 PNG cards. The full frame is retained without crop, enlargement, or burned-in captions. Video production remains separate from application Meta model calls.
