# Participant task — six-minute creator session

This sheet is ready for a real adult writer, worldbuilder, or concept artist. It contains no participant answers or results.

## Before the timer

The facilitator reads:

> We are trying an early scenario-creation tool, not testing you. The saved stories are counterfactuals. Their sources support starting-world facts; the changed paths are imagined. The 3D object is an illustration. You can skip a question or stop at any time. May I take anonymized notes about what you do and say? Separately, may we publish an anonymized quotation? There is no need to record your voice or screen.

Record the two consent choices. If note-taking consent is declined, do not create a scored record. Do not request a name, email, employer, or other identifying information.

## Read this task, then start the timer

> You are preparing a concept for your own short story, world, or artwork. Starting from this page, open a saved what-if that interests you. Choose one path and compare another. Use one linked source to describe a starting-world fact and name the source. Identify the changed assumption, explain two imagined steps, and name one uncertainty. Inspect the map, scene, or 3D object and explain one concrete visual detail that helps or distracts from your concept. Save a scenario brief, open it, and check whether it preserves the source, assumption, and uncertainty. You have about six minutes. Please say what you are thinking; ask for help whenever you need it.

The facilitator observes without navigating or explaining the answers. Record any help given. At six minutes, record completion state and elapsed time; report any later completion separately.

## After the task

Ask these questions without supplying the desired answer:

1. What does the linked source establish, and what does it leave unverified about this scenario?
2. Which visual detail helped or distracted, and what would you change before using this brief in your own creative work?

Record actions, participant statements, and facilitator interpretations separately in the [session record](SESSION_TEMPLATE.md). The [five-item rubric and 3–5 participant target](../USER_VALIDATION.md#success-rubric) remain authoritative. An agent rehearsal does not count as a creator session.
